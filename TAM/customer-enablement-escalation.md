# Customer Enablement, Product Context and Engineering Escalation

## Enablement
Discover customer goals, stakeholders, workflows and constraints. Agree success criteria, architecture, data flows, dependencies, milestones, owners and dates. Validate integrations in non-production, train admins/users, complete UAT and hand over support paths. Measure first value and adoption.

## Technical value
Connect technical activity to business measures such as cycle time, manual effort, processing volume, error rate, completion rate, productivity or risk reduction. Define baseline, target, source, period and owner before claiming impact.

## Product context recorded in Notion
The notes describe mortgage technology workflows, including Fannie Mae-related income workflows and product references such as Fannie Mae Income Calculator, Upfront Income, Upfront Index and Upfront AUS. Business-channel terms include TPO, wholesale, consumer-direct, retail and correspondent. Treat this as source-note context, not independently verified product positioning or performance claims. Confirm current product documentation before external use.

Integration discovery should cover LOS/client systems, API contract, authentication, data mapping, sync/async behavior, retries, performance, error handling, testing and operational ownership. Do not disclose customer names, real payloads, internal endpoints or secrets.

## Engineering escalation packet
**Impact + Scope + Environment + Expected/Actual + Reproduction + Evidence + Tests + Priority + Next update.** Record severity, affected workflows, start time/timezone, product/build, tenant/environment, reproduction steps, correlation ID, redacted logs/traces/HAR, recent changes, tests completed, workaround risk, engineering owner and customer-update owner.

## Customer communication
Acknowledge impact, distinguish known facts from hypotheses, state action/owner and next update time. Do not promise an unconfirmed resolution date. Own the communication loop through customer confirmation and KB update.
