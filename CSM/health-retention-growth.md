# Customer Health, Retention and Expansion

## Health dimensions
| Dimension | Signals |
|---|---|
| Adoption | Core workflow use, active users, feature depth |
| Engagement | Meeting and stakeholder participation |
| Outcomes | KPI progress and success-plan milestones |
| Sentiment | CSAT, NPS and qualitative feedback |
| Support | Volume, severity and recurring incidents |
| Relationships | Champion strength, executive access, multi-threading |
| Commercial | Renewal timing and contract context |

Weight signals by product, segment and lifecycle. Use trends, define thresholds and action playbooks, and validate automated scores with customer context. A green score must not override obvious qualitative risk.

## At-risk workflow
**Detect → Diagnose → Align → Act → Measure → Escalate.** Investigate product issues, leadership changes, poor onboarding, low adoption, budget pressure, changed priorities and competitive threats. Agree a recovery plan with owners, dates and measurable milestones.

## Retention
Renewal should be the conclusion of demonstrated value, not a surprise event. Establish success criteria during onboarding, monitor adoption/outcomes, maintain multi-threaded relationships and review risks continuously.

## Expansion
Expansion should be value-led. Look for adjacent workflows, unmet needs, increased scale or new teams only after validating existing value. Discover the problem and fit before involving the commercial owner. Do not present roadmap items as committed functionality or promise unapproved pricing.
