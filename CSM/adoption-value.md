# Product Adoption and Value Realization

## Adoption dimensions
- Breadth: proportion of relevant users adopting.
- Depth: use of relevant features and workflows.
- Frequency: how often workflows are completed.
- Quality: whether usage is correct and effective.
- Outcome: whether usage creates business value.

Do not optimize for logins alone. Enable the right personas to complete the right workflows.

## Value realization
1. Reconfirm the customer's desired business outcome.
2. Establish baseline and agreed KPI.
3. Map product workflows to the outcome.
4. Identify adoption barriers by persona and workflow.
5. Agree enablement actions and owners.
6. Measure progress over an agreed period.
7. Share evidence and adjust the success plan.

## Measures
Depending on use case: cycle time, manual effort, processing volume, error rate, completion rate, conversion, cost, risk or productivity. Document metric definition, source, baseline, target, period and owner.

High usage does not automatically mean value. Investigate business goals, workflow quality, stakeholder sentiment and ROI.
