# Onboarding and Time to Value

## Framework: Goal → People → Plan → Product → Proof → Handoff

1. **Goal:** Define business outcome, baseline and target. “Reduce manual processing time by an agreed amount within 60 days” is stronger than “implement the platform.”
2. **People:** Identify economic buyer, champion, executive sponsor, administrator, end users, technical stakeholders and blockers.
3. **Plan:** Document milestones, owners, dates, dependencies, risks, communication cadence and acceptance criteria.
4. **Product:** Configure and train around real workflows; validate integrations, data, permissions and prerequisites.
5. **Proof:** Agree evidence of first value, such as a completed workflow, reduced cycle time or fewer errors.
6. **Handoff:** Record goals, stakeholders, configuration, usage, open issues, risks, commitments, support paths and next milestones.

## Metrics
- TTV: time until meaningful customer value is realized.
- TTFV: time until the first meaningful benefit.
- Launch time, milestone attainment, training completion and onboarding completion.

Define start/end events consistently. Technical implementation completion is not necessarily customer value.

## Risk controls
Track dependency owners and dates. Escalate blocked milestones early. Do not promise dates owned by another team without confirmation.
