# CSM Metrics and Interview Preparation

## Core metrics
- CSAT: satisfied responses ÷ total valid responses × 100; scales/thresholds vary.
- NPS: % Promoters (9–10) − % Detractors (0–6); Passives are 7–8.
- TTV/TTFV: time to meaningful value / first meaningful value.
- ARR/MRR: annual/monthly recurring revenue.
- Logo churn: customer accounts lost over a defined period.
- GRR: (starting recurring revenue − churn − contraction) ÷ starting recurring revenue × 100. Excludes expansion and is generally capped at 100%.
- NRR: (starting recurring revenue − churn − contraction + expansion) ÷ starting recurring revenue × 100. Can exceed 100%.

Always define period, denominator, cohort, exclusions and company-specific metric conventions.

## Competencies
Customer empathy, communication, ownership, problem solving, product understanding, business acumen, data orientation, stakeholder management, commercial thinking and proactivity.

## Structured interview answer
Customer problem → business goal → diagnosis → action plan → measurable result → proactive next step.

For behavioral examples use Situation / Goal / Action / Result / Lesson. Use genuine evidence; never invent metrics or claim sole ownership of cross-functional work.

## Scenario frameworks
- Angry customer: Listen → Acknowledge → Clarify → Own → Plan → Follow through.
- Churn risk: Detect → Diagnose → Align → Act → Measure → Escalate.
- Prioritization: risk × impact × urgency × opportunity.
- Feature request: customer → problem → use case → impact → frequency → evidence → desired outcome.
- QBR: goals → progress → evidence → outcomes → risks → next-quarter plan.

Prepare real examples covering difficult customers, retention, churn risk, onboarding, product issues, escalations, stakeholder conflict, process improvement, prioritization and executive conversations.
