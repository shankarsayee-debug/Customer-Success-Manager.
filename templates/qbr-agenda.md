# QBR / EBR Agenda

1. Customer priorities and business goals.
2. Success-plan progress and KPI outcomes.
3. Adoption and workflow performance.
4. Value delivered and evidence.
5. Wins and lessons.
6. Open issues, support themes and risks.
7. Strategic recommendations.
8. Relevant adjacent needs/opportunities.
9. Next-quarter objectives and milestones.
10. Actions, owners and dates.

## Pre-read checklist
- Confirm attendees and stakeholder roles.
- Validate KPI definitions, baseline and actuals.
- Review adoption trends and support history.
- Confirm open commitments and risks.
- Separate measured outcomes from estimates.
